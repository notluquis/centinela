# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right, including when they are wrong. Argue with the claim and not with whoever made it — which is also the rule this repository applies to its own history, where several confident claims turned out to be false and were corrected in place rather than quietly deleted.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind. Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission. Sustained disruption of a discussion.

That applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and anywhere else this project is being discussed.

## Reporting

Open an issue if it can be public. If it cannot, the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) reaches the same person and is the right one to use.

Reports are read by the maintainer, who will respond, and who may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts.

## Scope

This applies to anyone taking part, maintainer included. There is one maintainer, which means there is nobody to appeal to — worth saying plainly rather than implying a process that does not exist.

## Attribution

The substance is the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, rewritten shorter for a project this size. Where the wording differs the Covenant's intent is what is meant.
